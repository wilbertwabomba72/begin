Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

Bingo 90 Number Generator is designed as an offline single-player bingo game. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information. The app does not require an account, email address, phone number, name, location, contacts, photos, microphone access, camera access, or advertising identifier.

Local Game Data

The app may store gameplay information locally on your device, such as unlocked levels, stars, best results, sound settings, music settings, vibration settings, and voice callout settings. This information stays on your device and is used only to run the game and remember your preferences.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

The current app does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking technologies, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell or share data with advertisers or data brokers.

Network Use

The game is intended to work offline. It does not send gameplay data to our servers.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not collect personal data, no child personal information is stored or transmitted by the app.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored game progress and settings remain on your device until you reset the app data, uninstall the app, or delete the app from your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app changes. Any updated version should be made available through the app's privacy policy page or App Store listing.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the support contact provided on the App Store product page.
